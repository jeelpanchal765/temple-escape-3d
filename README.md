# 🏃‍♂️ Temple Escape 3D - Ancient Ruins 3D Endless Runner

A fast-paced, high-performance 3D endless runner game built with **HTML5 & Three.js (WebGL)** inspired by the classic feel of **Temple Run 2**, featuring authentic canyon temple ruins, responsive 3-lane physics, power-ups, dynamic demon chasing AI, and Web Audio synthesized soundscapes.

## 🎮 Key Features

- **Smooth 3-Lane Mechanics**: Responsive lane switching with realistic banking and tilting.
- **Dynamic Physics & Actions**:
  - Parabolic jumps with variable height and landing dust shockwaves.
  - Low-profile slides under stone beams and roots with sparks and dust trails.
  - In-air fast-fall cancelling directly into slides.
- **Fair Procedural Track & Obstacles**:
  - 🪵 **Spiked Logs**: Jump over hazardous fallen timber.
  - 🏛️ **Ancient Stone Archways**: Slide under low temple beams and roots.
  - 🪨 **Mossy Boulders & Broken Stumps**: Dodge lane hazards.
  - 🔥 **Fire Braziers & Lava Pits**: Leap or steer clear of raging flames.
  - 🕳️ **Canyon Chasms & Broken Bridges**: Jump across deep abyss gaps.
  - ⚔️ **Swinging Pendulums**: Time your slide under swinging temple blades.
- **Dynamic Chasing Demon Beast AI**:
  - The Cursed Demon Beast bounds right behind you.
  - Stumbling or clipping obstacles brings the beast roaring to your neck.
  - Running cleanly allows you to pull away into safety.
- **Power-ups & Collectibles**:
  - 🛡️ **Tiki Shield**: Absorbs a fatal collision and shatters safely.
  - 🧲 **Coin Magnet**: Drags coins across all 3 lanes directly to the player.
  - 🪽 **Supersonic Flame Wings**: Invincible flight, speed boost, and obstacle clearing.
  - ✖️2 **Score Multiplier**: Doubles distance score and coin values.
  - 👹 **Demon Idol Meter**: Collect gems to fill the Idol gauge and unleash Supersonic Boost!
  - 🔥 **Coin Combo Multiplier**: Streak coin collections to build up to $5\times$ multiplier rewards.
- **Web Audio Sound Synthesizer**:
  - Dynamic adaptive jungle drum rhythms that match your running speed.
  - Melodic coin pickup chimes with pitch progression on combos.
  - Distinct jump whooshes, slide stone scraping, demon roars, shield break effects, and 3-2-1 countdown beeps.
- **Temple Run 2 Authentic HUD**:
  - Carved stone plaques, glowing counters, live power-up timers, and 3-2-1 countdown.
- **Instant Restart & Persistence**:
  - Seamless restart without browser reloading.
  - Best score, distance, and total gems saved to `localStorage`.

## 🕹️ Controls

| Action | Desktop Keyboard | Mobile Touch / Swipe |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Move Left** | `A` or `←` | Swipe Left / Tap `◀` |
| **Move Right** | `D` or `→` | Swipe Right / Tap `▶` |
| **Jump** | `W` / `↑` / `Space` | Swipe Up / Tap `▲` |
| **Slide** | `S` / `↓` | Swipe Down / Tap `▼` |
| **Supersonic Boost** | `E` or Tap Idol | Tap Demon Idol Icon |
| **Pause / Resume** | `P` or `ESC` | Tap Pause Button |
